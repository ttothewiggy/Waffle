Waffle PDF fonts are bundled locally for private/offline export.
Libre Baskerville, Noto Sans and Kalam originate from their @fontsource packages.
Original SIL Open Font Licenses and copyright notices accompany these files.

WaffleHandOblique is a modified Kalam face for PDF italic rendering: contours
are sheared by 0.18, the italic flag is set and the family is renamed.
It remains distributed under Kalam's SIL Open Font License.
